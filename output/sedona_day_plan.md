# 1-day plan for Sedona

_Built from Wikivoyage listings by trip-scout._

## Morning - See

- **[Palatki Heritage Site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/coconino/recarea/?recid=55368)** - A Sinagua archeological site with cliff dwellings and pictographs and petroglyphs, dated to 1100-1400 CE. The site is located in the Coconino National Forest, and has three interpretive trails. Reservations are recommended, as visitor numbers are managed by the rangers to limit damage to the site.… _(Hours: Daily 9:30AM-3PM; Price: Red Rock Pass)_

## Lunch - Eat

- **[Picazzo Pizza](http://www.pizzapicazzo.com)** - Very good food, pizza choices are too numerous to mention. Take out available. _(Hours: Sun-Thu 11AM-9PM, Fri Sat 11AM-10PM)_

## Afternoon - See

- **[Honanki Heritage Site](https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/coconino/recarea/?recid=55318)** - A Sinagua cliff dwelling site with numerous pictographs, inhabited from 1100-1300 CE. The site is managed by the Coconino National Forest Service. A Red Rock Pass is required on all vehicles; the passes are not sold here and must be purchased beforehand. _(Hours: Daily 9:30AM-4PM; Price: Red Rock Pass)_

## Evening - Do

- **[Red Rock State Park](https://azstateparks.com/red-rock)** - This 286-acre preserve was once part of the Smoke Trail Ranch, and has ten well-maintained hiking trails as well as a visitor center. A number of guided hikes, birdwatching walks, and astronomy events are offered. _(Hours: Daily 8AM-5PM; Price: $10/vehicle (up to 4 adults))_
