# 1-day plan for Flagstaff

_Built from Wikivoyage listings by trip-scout._

## Morning - See

- **[Lowell Observatory](http://www.lowell.edu/)** - An astronomical research center from which the dwarf planet Pluto was discovered, this historic institution is on a mesa overlooking the west side of town. The staff leads guided daytime tours, and visitors use the Observatory's 24" Clark telescope and smaller portable telescopes during regular… _(Hours: Daily 9AM-10PM; Price: $12 (adults), $10 (students/seniors), $5 (children 5-17))_

## Lunch - Eat

- **[Hunan East](http://www.hunaneastflagstaff.com/)** - Great Chinese food in a casual atmosphere. Takeout available. _(Hours: Sun-Thu 11AM-8:45PM, Fri Sat 11AM-9PM)_

## Afternoon - See

- **[Museum of Northern Arizona](http://www.musnaz.org/)** - On the north side of town, it has displays on Native American life and the natural history of the region. A good selection of Native American folk art, particularly Navajo rugs, is available at the gift shop. _(Hours: Daily 9AM-5PM except some holidays; Price: $15 (adults))_

## Evening - Do

- **[Arizona Snowbowl](http://www.arizonasnowbowl.com)** - Alpine skiing. One of only three ski resorts in the state of Arizona. Despite the surrounding desert, Flagstaff is a popular skiing destination for the Southwest due to the high elevation.
